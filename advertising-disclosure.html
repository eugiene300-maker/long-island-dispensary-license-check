Advertising disclosure

How this free tool is funded — in plain English.

Sponsored placement

This site is free to use and is supported by advertising. The "Dispensary of the Week" feature, the sponsor ribbon at the top of each page, and the sponsor box in the footer are paid placements. They are always labeled "Sponsored." The current sponsor is Planet Nugg Weed Dispensary & Delivery (Farmingdale, NY; OCM license OCM-CAURD-24-000069).

What sponsorship does not buy

  • Sponsors cannot change, hide or reorder anyone's license information.
  • Sponsors cannot remove competitors from the directory or search results.
  • Sponsors must hold an active, operating New York cannabis license. We will never feature an unlicensed business.

Links

Links to sponsors are marked with rel="sponsored". Some links may include tracking parameters so sponsors can see visits that came from this site. We don't earn a commission on purchases.

Ratings

Google ratings shown for featured dispensaries are copied from the business's public Google Business Profile on the date noted and may have changed since.

Advertising standards

This site is intended for adults 21+ and uses an age confirmation screen. Cannabis warnings required under New York rules appear in the footer of every page.
