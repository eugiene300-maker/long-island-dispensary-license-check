Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and your choices.

Effective: September 11, 2026

Summary

You can use the license lookup without creating an account or giving us any personal information. Searches run in your browser against a public dataset. We only collect personal information you choose to send us — your email if you join the newsletter, or your details if you contact us.

Information we collect

  • Newsletter: your email address, and your votes if you take part in Dispensary of the Week polls.
  • Contact form: your name, email and message.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider may log standard information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited for security and performance. We don't use this to identify you.
  • Local storage: we store a small flag in your browser so you aren't asked to confirm your age on every visit. It expires after 30 days.

How we use it

To send the newsletter you asked for, count votes, reply to messages, and keep the site secure. We don't sell or rent your personal information.

Service providers

We use third-party services to host the site, deliver email and process form submissions. The Dispensary of the Week map is provided by Google Maps, which may set its own cookies when it loads — see Google's privacy policy. Searches that find no Long Island match may query New York's public Open Data API (data.ny.gov) from your browser.

Your choices

Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. You can ask us to access or delete your information at any time by emailing hello@longislanddispensarycheck.com.

Age restriction

This site is intended only for adults 21 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 21; if we learn we have, we'll delete it.

Changes

We'll post any changes to this policy on this page and update the effective date.
