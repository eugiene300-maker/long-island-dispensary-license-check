Methodology & data

Where our data comes from, what each status means, and how often it's refreshed.

Data source

All license information comes from the Current OCM Licenses dataset published by the New York State Office of Cannabis Management (OCM) on the Open Data NY portal. We include every record in Nassau and Suffolk counties with one of these license types:

  • Adult-Use Conditional Retail Dispensary (CAURD)
  • Adult-Use Retail Dispensary
  • Adult-Use Microbusiness
  • Registered Organization and Registered Organization Dispensary (medical)

Cultivator, processor and distributor licenses are excluded because those businesses don't sell to the public.

Update schedule

An automated job runs every Monday morning. It downloads the latest records from OCM's public API, rebuilds our Long Island dataset, and republishes the site. If the state's API is unavailable or returns incomplete data, the job stops and the previous week's data stays live. The "Updated" date at the top of the homepage shows the last successful check. Our full dataset is available at /data/licenses.json.

What each status means

  • Licensed · Open — OCM lists the license as active and the location as operating.
  • Licensed · Medical — an active Registered Organization license to dispense medical cannabis.
  • Licensed · Not open yet — the license is active, but OCM lists the location as non-operational. It shouldn't be selling to the public yet.
  • Application pending — OCM shows an application in process. The business is not yet licensed.
  • License inactive — the license is no longer active.
  • No license found — we couldn't match the name to any Long Island record. This isn't proof a shop is unlicensed (names on licenses sometimes differ from storefront signs), so we recommend checking OCM directly.

How search matching works

Search checks each record's trade name (DBA), legal entity name, street address, town and license number. It tolerates partial words and small typos, and ignores filler words like "LLC," "Inc." and "Dispensary." If nothing matches on Long Island, we also query OCM's statewide data live and show any matches elsewhere in New York.

"Opened to public" dates

Opening dates come from OCM's "retail date opened to public" field. Some operating licensees haven't reported a date; we show "Date not reported" in those cases.

Dispensary of the Week

Dispensary of the Week is a sponsored placement. To be eligible, a dispensary must (1) hold an active, operating OCM license, and (2) meet a customer-satisfaction threshold of at least a 4.5-star average Google rating across 100+ reviews at the time of selection. Ratings are shown with the date they were recorded. Newsletter subscribers can vote for future featured dispensaries. Sponsorship has no effect on license data, search results, or directory inclusion.

Corrections

We republish state records as-is. If you believe a record is wrong, please tell OCM and let us know — we'll flag it while it's reviewed.
