About this tool

A free, independent way to confirm a Long Island dispensary is licensed before you buy.

Why we built it

Long Island has licensed cannabis dispensaries — and plenty of unlicensed shops that look just like them. From the sidewalk, it's hard to tell the difference. New York publishes every cannabis license as open data, but that data isn't easy for shoppers to search on a phone at the store's front door.

Long Island Dispensary License Check turns that public record into a fast, simple lookup focused on Nassau and Suffolk counties. Type a store's name and you'll see its license number, license type, status, and the date it opened to the public — straight from the state's records.

What we do (and don't do)

  • We do republish New York State Office of Cannabis Management license records for Long Island, refreshed automatically every week.
  • We do explain what each license status means in plain English.
  • We don't sell cannabis, take orders, or process payments.
  • We don't rate or rank dispensaries on license status — every licensed store appears in our directory at no cost.

How the site is funded

The tool is free to use. It's supported by a clearly labeled sponsored placement, "Dispensary of the Week." Sponsorship never changes the license data or search results — a sponsor's license record is displayed exactly as the state publishes it. Read the full advertising disclosure.

Independence

This website is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the New York State Office of Cannabis Management or any government agency.
