    Guide

    Visiting a licensed dispensary on Long Island

    Verify before you buy

    Licensed stores sell products that are tracked, lab-tested and labeled under New York rules. Unlicensed "smoke shops" aren't inspected — and what's in the jar is anyone's guess.

    Mostly in Suffolk

    Most Nassau County municipalities opted out of adult-use retail, so Long Island's licensed storefronts cluster in Suffolk towns like Farmingdale, Deer Park, Medford, Riverhead and the Hamptons.

    Shop smarter

    Many licensed dispensaries post live menus, first-time-customer discounts and weekly deals online, with in-store pickup and delivery across Long Island.

    Long Island's legal cannabis market has grown quickly since New York's first adult-use dispensaries opened. Today you'll find licensed stores selling flower, edibles, concentrates, vape cartridges, pre-rolls, topicals and more — alongside a stubborn number of unlicensed shops that look legitimate from the street. This tool exists to make it easy to tell the two apart.

    How to check if a Long Island dispensary is licensed

    1. Search the name above. Partial names work — type "nugg" or "strain" and pick from the suggestions. You can also search by street, town or license number.
    2. Read the status. "Licensed · Open" means New York lists the store as licensed and operating. "Not open yet," "Application pending" or "Inactive" means the business shouldn't be selling cannabis right now.
    3. Look for the sign. Every licensed dispensary must post OCM's Dispensary Verification Tool — a sign with a QR code — near its main entrance. Scan it to confirm.

    Ordering from a licensed Long Island dispensary

    You can see what's in stock without leaving home. Most licensed Long Island dispensaries offer online menus where you can browse products, apply deals or first-time discounts, and order ahead for in-store pickup or delivery. Some also offer curbside pickup. Bring a valid ID showing you're 21 or older — you'll be carded at the door and again at pickup or delivery.

    FAQ

    Long Island dispensary questions, answered

    Is weed legal on Long Island?

    Yes. Adult-use (recreational) and medical cannabis are both legal in Nassau and Suffolk counties under New York State law. However, many Long Island towns and villages opted out of hosting adult-use retail stores, so licensed storefronts are concentrated in the Suffolk County communities that allowed them. According to OCM's license data, no adult-use retail dispensaries are licensed in Nassau County; Nassau's licensed locations are medical dispensaries run by Registered Organizations.

    How can I tell if a Long Island dispensary is licensed?

    Type the store's name into the search bar at the top of this page. We match it against New York's official Current OCM Licenses dataset and show the license number, license type, and the date the store opened to the public. In person, every licensed dispensary must post OCM's Dispensary Verification Tool — a sign with a scannable QR code — near its main entrance.

    What if a dispensary doesn't show up in the search?

    Try the street address, the town, or the business's legal name, since some stores trade under a name that differs from the one on their license. If you still can't find it, treat the shop as unverified and check OCM's official dispensary verification list before buying. Unlicensed shops are not inspected and their products are not required to be lab-tested.

    What does CAURD mean?

    CAURD stands for Conditional Adult-Use Retail Dispensary. It was New York's first adult-use retail license, reserved for justice-involved entrepreneurs and nonprofits. A CAURD license is a full, legal license to sell cannabis to adults 21+. Newer stores hold a standard Adult-Use Retail Dispensary license.

    How old do you have to be to buy cannabis on Long Island?

    You must be 21 or older to buy from an adult-use dispensary, and you'll need a valid government-issued photo ID or passport. Medical cannabis requires a valid New York medical cannabis certification and registry ID; patients under 18 need a registered parent, guardian, or caregiver.

    Are Long Island dispensaries cash only?

    Many are, so bring cash. Because cannabis is still illegal under federal law, most dispensaries can't process credit cards. Some accept debit cards, cashless ATM transactions, or ACH payments — call ahead or check the store's website.

    Does health insurance cover medical cannabis?

    Generally no. Most U.S. health insurance plans don't cover medical cannabis because it remains federally illegal.

    Can tourists buy from a Long Island dispensary?

    Yes. Any adult 21 or older with valid ID can buy from a licensed adult-use dispensary in New York, including visitors from out of state or out of the country.

    What's the difference between adult-use and medical cannabis?

    Adult-use (recreational) cannabis can be purchased by any adult 21+ without a medical reason. Medical cannabis is purchased by certified patients at medical dispensaries operated by Registered Organizations, often with different products, pricing, and tax treatment.

    How often is this data updated?

    Automatically every week. Our system pulls the latest records from the NY Office of Cannabis Management's open-data license dataset and republishes the site. The date of the most recent check is shown on this page.
